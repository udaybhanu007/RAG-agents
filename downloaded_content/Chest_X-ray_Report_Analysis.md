## Step 1: Analyze the Report (Detailed Findings)

Radiologists systematically evaluate the chest X-ray in the following order:

1. **Technical Quality**
   * PA/AP view, rotation, exposure, inspiratory effort.
   
2. **Airways**
   * Trachea: central or shifted?
   * Main bronchi: visible and open?

3. **Bones & Soft Tissues**
   * Ribs, clavicles, vertebrae: intact?
   * Any fractures, deformities, bone lesions?

4. **Cardiac Silhouette**
   * Normal heart size (cardiothoracic ratio < 50% in PA view).
   * Shape and borders clear?

5. **Mediastinum & Great Vessels**
   * Widened mediastinum? Aortic aneurysm? Lymph node enlargement?

6. **Lungs**
   * Symmetry of lung fields.
   * Look for **opacities** (infection, tumor, collapse).
   * Signs of **hyperinflation** (COPD, asthma).
   * Vascular markings: normal or increased (heart failure).

7. **Pleura & Diaphragm**
   * Pleural effusion (fluid), pneumothorax (air), thickening.
   * Diaphragm: domes smooth, right side slightly higher than left.

8. **Devices/Foreign Bodies**
   * Pacemaker, chest tubes, central venous lines, endotracheal tubes.

---

📝 **Step 2: Write the Summary (Impression)**

The **summary (impression)** is the most important part of the report. 

* Is **concise** (1–3 lines).
* Highlights the **key abnormal or normal findings**.
* Provides a likely **diagnosis or differential diagnosis**.

---

### ✅ Examples of Summaries

* **Normal Report:**  
  *“Normal chest X-ray. Clear lung fields, normal heart size, no evidence of pleural effusion or pneumothorax.”*
  
* **Pneumonia:**  
  *“Right lower zone consolidation suggestive of pneumonia. No pleural effusion or cardiomegaly.”*
  
* **Heart Failure:**  
  *“Cardiomegaly with bilateral pulmonary edema and small pleural effusions, consistent with congestive heart failure.”*
  
* **Pneumothorax:**  
  *“Left-sided pneumothorax with partial collapse of the lung. No mediastinal shift.”*
  
* **Post-surgery device check:**  
  *“Endotracheal tube tip in satisfactory position. Central venous catheter tip at SVC-RA junction. Clear lung fields.”*
